The arena

Fifteen seats, one model, and the resolution it writes for itself.

十五个席位，一个模型，以及它自己写出的决议。

Given the events up to a real Council meeting, a language model plays every member: the penholder drafts, the others negotiate and amend, all fifteen vote and explain their votes. The meeting it holds is its own and need not follow the real one. What we compare is where it ends up — the provisions of the final text and each member's final vote — and we read the gap as the model's own leanings.

给定一次真实安理会会议之前发生的事件，由一个语言模型扮演全部理事国：执笔国起草，其余各国磋商、提出修改，十五国表决并解释投票。 它开的是自己的会，不必与真实会议一样。我们只对照会议落在哪里——最终文本的条款和各国的最终投票——并把差距当作模型自身的倾向来读。
